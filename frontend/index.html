04 / LEARNING IN ACTION

Real projects.
Real possibilities.

Explore what students can build with creativity, technology and hands-on learning.

01 / ROBOTICS

Obstacle-Avoiding Robot

Project: Build a robot car that detects obstacles and changes direction automatically.

Real-world use: Introduces autonomous robots used in warehouses and industries.

02 / CODING

Interactive Maze Game

Project: Create a game using movement, conditions, events and scoring.

Real-world use: Builds logical thinking and introduces game development.

03 / ELECTRONICS

Automatic Street Light

Project: Use a light sensor to switch an LED on when the surroundings become dark.

Real-world use: Demonstrates automatic lighting and energy-saving systems.

04 / MECHANICS

Gears and Pulley Machine

Project: Build a model to explore gears, pulleys, motion and force.

Real-world use: Understand mechanisms found in cranes, elevators and machines.

05 / ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

AI Image Classifier

Project: Train a simple AI model to recognise selected images or objects.

Real-world use: Introduces image recognition used in accessibility and visual inspection.

06 / APP DEVELOPMENT

Student Utility App

Project: Design an app for reminders, quizzes or classroom activities.

Real-world use: Shows how apps can organise information and solve everyday problems.

07 / DESIGN THINKING

Eco-Friendly Product Prototype

Project: Design a prototype that addresses a problem such as plastic waste.

Real-world use: Develops creativity, prototyping and problem-solving skills.
